Congratulations to Year 13 student Nomeneta Harema, whose exceptional printmaking artwork, *Fa'asinomaga*, created in Mr Mac's class, has been selected for the Pat Hanly Creativity Awards Exhibition 2026.

Adding to this remarkable achievement, Nomeneta's artwork has also been chosen for the exhibition's promotional material, recognising both the quality of her work and her artistic talent.

The exhibition will be held at Auckland Art Gallery Toi o Tāmaki from 8 August to 13 September 2026, with the opening event, Young Gallery Night, taking place on 7 August from 6.00pm to 9.00pm.

We're also proud to celebrate the creativity of our junior artists. Our Semester 1 Art Exhibition is now on display in the D6 foyer, featuring a wonderful collection of work by our Year 9, 10 and 11 students. We encourage our school community to stop by and enjoy the talent on display.

Ka rawe, Nomeneta! We're incredibly proud of your achievement and wish you every success at the exhibition.
