Rosehill Intermediate School is now accepting **Year 7 enrolment applications for 2027**.

The intermediate years are an important stage in every student's learning journey. At Rosehill Intermediate School, students are supported in a safe and engaging environment where they are encouraged to grow academically, socially, and personally while preparing for a successful transition to high school.

Families are warmly invited to learn more about the school's curriculum, specialist learning opportunities, sports, and extracurricular programmes at the upcoming **Year 6 Parent Information Evening**.

### **Key Dates**

* **20 July** – Year 7 enrolments open
* **5 August** – Year 6 Parent Information Evening

  **5.30 pm** in the School Hall
* **2 September** – Out-of-zone applications close
* **16 September** – Year 6 Orientation Morning

  **9.30 am** in the School Hall

For further information, please contact **[office@rosehill.org.nz](mailto:office@rosehill.org.nz)**.

Rosehill Intermediate School encourage families that are considering them for 2027 to attend the information evening and find out more about the opportunities available for your child.
