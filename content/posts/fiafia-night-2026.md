Come along for an evening of culture, music, dance and celebration at Fiafia Night 2026.

Our Rosehill College students will take the stage to share their cultures and talents through performances, colourful costumes and plenty of energy. It's a great opportunity for whānau, friends and our wider community to come together and support our students.

**📅 Friday 21 August 2026**
**🕕 6.00pm**
**📍 The Hall, Rosehill College**
**🪙 $2 entry**

Everyone is welcome. Come along, support our students and enjoy the evening with us.
