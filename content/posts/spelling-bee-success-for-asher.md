A big congratulations to Year 10 student Asher, who has qualified for the Aotearoa New Zealand National Spelling Bee Auckland Regional Final in September.

That puts Asher among the top 350 spellers in the country — a fantastic achievement. He'll now compete for a place in the national finals in Wellington next month.

All the best, Asher! We'll be cheering you on at the Regional Final. 🐝📚
